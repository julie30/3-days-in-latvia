Practical Tips

Quick tips to make traveling around Latvia easier.

Transport

It is convenient to travel by car. In cities, pay attention to parking areas. There is a fee to enter Jurmala by car.

Budget

Prices in Euros. Accommodation is cheaper during the off-season than in the summer.

When to travel?

Summer is great for the seaside, and autumn and spring are for cities and nature (fewer people).

What to taste?

It is worth trying Latvian bread, desserts, and local cuisine in cafes.
